Editor introductions

AYAKA

Writes about food and shopping, where to go on weekends, limited-time events, and life in Japan. From Fukushima, has lived in Tokyo for more than 20 years.

How she writes

Which shopping street has not yet been packed with tourists, which shop's seasonal ingredients have just arrived, her radar is faster than a food show. She speaks directly: first what to buy, how much it costs, and where to buy it, then matches it to usage situations. Looking like a good deal in the store does not mean you will use it every day at home. She organizes official information and in-store rules into a checklist you can use before buying. Every article ends by returning to her signature question: Will this really be useful for living in Japan?

At the editorial office

Staff member No. 2 holds the company record for submitting on time. For her drafts, Choko usually replies with just one line: "This one is fine." YU has always been unconvinced about that.
