Editor introductions

腸鍋

The editor-in-chief, that is, the boss of this editorial office. A Japanese uncle who loves running, responsible for the final check of every piece of information, and the running beat is also his to write.

How he checks and gates quality

When YU and AYAKA submit their drafts, 腸鍋 asks three things first: Are the sources correct, will people living in Japan actually use this, and are any details missing? He looks half asleep, but his memory is excellent, and he remembers exactly what was wrong in whose draft.

When it passes, he says only one thing: “This one is fine. Do not embarrass me out there.” When it does not pass, he sends it back: “Rewrite it. This is not something people who live here would read.”

What he is like at the company

Always the last to arrive at meetings, always the first to finish reading a draft. He never takes off his waist pouch, and no one knows what's inside. Ask him, and he just replies: "It's a secret."

Columns responsible for

All sections. Life Information, Weekend Outings, Food & Shopping, Housing, Work, Chinese in Japan, and Limited-Time Events all go through him in the end.

He also posts himself, writing about running: running spots in Tokyo and the ins and outs of morning runs; the running beat is his. The material comes from what he has run with his own two feet.
