Each shop sets its own 2027 osechi deadline, and two of them fall at the end of November

Seven-Eleven early bird ends November 30, Takashimaya convenience store payment closes November 28, and the three department store groups close online orders between December 21 and 25

Short on time? The summary at the end lists all four shops' deadlines in order, so scroll down, check the dates, then come back and take your time choosing.

By mid November, osechi catalogues appear at supermarket entrances, the tiered boxes lined up neatly on the covers, the golden colour of kuri kinton visible even through the paper. People spending their first New Year in Japan often pause in front of this shelf. Ordering itself is easy. The hard part is that every shop has a different deadline, and if you only remember in mid December, the box you liked may already be closed for orders. There is no time to dawdle.

The closest gate is Seven-Eleven. Eligible items reserved in the 7NOW app for store pickup get 10 percent off if the reservation is completed by 23:59 on November 30, 2026, and reservations made on the in-store form are not included. Regular reservations run to December 28 in store and to 10:29 on December 29 in 7NOW, with pickup concentrated on December 30 and 31. Cancellation is possible until each item's reservation deadline, but quantities and contents cannot be changed. If you want a different box, you cancel and reserve again, and whether it is still in stock depends on the moment.

Takashimaya's online deadline looks like the latest, but a much earlier date hides inside it. Online orders run in principle to 10:00 on December 25, 2026, some items to 10:00 on December 27, and credit cards and electronic payments follow that date. 🍱 For people paying at a convenience store, by online banking or with Pay-easy, the deadline is 18:00 on November 28, almost a month earlier. Pick the wrong payment method and that December 25 date has nothing to do with you. After ordering, cancellations, exchanges and delivery address changes are in principle not accepted, so a wrongly written address cannot be fixed.

Most items on the Mitsukoshi Isetan online store are available until 10:00 AM on December 21, 2026. The Daimaru Matsuzakaya Online Store is open until 10:00 AM on December 22, though those paying via convenience store, ATM, or online banking must complete their orders by December 16. Daimaru Matsuzakaya delivery dates are December 31, from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM; specific time slots cannot be chosen. As stated in their official FAQ, changes, cancellations, and returns are not accepted once an order is placed. These are the overall deadlines for each store; individual products, pickup dates, and delivery regions may have earlier cut-off times, so please check the date listed on the product page before ordering.

Servings are another easy place to go wrong. The number of servings on a product page is only a guide, and a household with hearty eaters who orders exactly per head often finds the New Year's Day table a little short. The editorial team has a record of this. We bought two or three servings of bento and side dishes thinking they would do for the next day too, and finished everything in that one meal. The next day the fridge held only empty containers. Osechi works the same way. A box marked for two to three people can be emptied on New Year's Day by two adults with good appetites, and the table over the first three days of the year grows thinner by the day.

For frozen items and delivery, it helps to walk through the plan in your head before ordering. A frozen osechi takes a corner of the freezer, and thawing takes about a day in the fridge. If your freezer is already full, measuring the space first means you will not be standing there on delivery day holding a heavy box with nowhere to put it. If you will be away at year end, choose a delivery day when you are home. Daimaru Matsuzakaya does not let you pick a time slot, so if you are out all day, store pickup works better. Seven-Eleven store pickup is on December 30 and 31, and collecting it on the way home from work actually makes the timing easier to control.

Does it really come in useful when you live in Japan? Put that question to osechi and the answer sits on the table over those two days. The lid of the tiered box opens, sweet aromas and pickled scents spread together, and the zoni is still steaming. The family finishes a meal together, and that is how the year begins. Every shop's deadline is different, so once the dates are lined up, all that remains is choosing a box that makes you happy to look at.

From the editor-in-chief

This article lines the dates up so neatly, and yet every year I still end up in the supermarket on the night of December 31, looking at empty shelves with half price stickers and thinking I am late again this year. No tiered box, just two packs of mochi, and I go oddly quiet on New Year's morning while cooking the zoni.
