A Taiwan license converts to a Japanese license without tests, while the 50 questions facing a China license got harder

From October 2025 the 知識確認 became 50 questions requiring 45 correct, and the pass rate fell to 42.8%. Tokyo residents should remember to book Fuchu or Samezu

If you only want to know whether you need to take the tests, the answer is in the 懶人包 at the end, just scroll down and check.

For people who live by train, a driver's license usually sits far down the to-do list. Wanting to borrow a car for a move, or to visit a big suburban store on the weekend, the thought surfaces, and only then do you notice that 外免切替 changed its face in October 2025. I have lived in Japan for years, I cannot drive, and my days run on trains. This piece organizes the new rules for friends who are converting their licenses.

The biggest change is the 知識確認. The 道路交通法施行規則 amendment took effect on October 1, 2025, and the 警視庁 revision notice states it plainly, illustration questions abolished, the count up from 10 to 50 questions, the pass line raised from 7 of 10 to 45 of 50, 90 percent to pass, held only once in the afternoon. The era of passing by looking at pictures and guessing is over. 🚗

The 技能確認 also tightened. Both the 警視庁 and 茨城県警 explanations mention new course tasks such as passing pedestrian crossings, grading aligned with the strictness of a brand new license test, and heavier deductions than before for failing to signal or turning incorrectly. The 警察庁 figures for the first three months after the revision, October to December 2025, put the 知識確認 pass rate at 42.8%, against 92.5% in 2024 before the revision. The 技能確認 rate was 13.1%, against 30.4% the previous year. Both numbers fell by more than half, and anyone going in with a just-give-it-a-try mindset is now likely to be sent home at the first gate.

Taiwan and mainland China licenses are treated differently on the list. The 29 countries and regions published by 警視庁 are exempt from both the 知識確認 and the 技能確認, and Taiwan is on that list. Mainland China is not on the list, so both checks must be taken. There is one shared threshold, after getting the license you must have stayed in the issuing country for a cumulative 3 months or more, and the entry and exit records in your passport are what prove it. The Japanese translation of a Taiwan license is made by the 台湾日本関係協会, and for a mainland China license the translation goes through bodies such as JAF listed by 警視庁 and the prefectural police. If the translation is not right, the counter will not take the application further.

The document gate also hardened after October 2025. 警視庁 requires a 住民票 with the specified items recorded, covering nationality, status of residence, period of stay and 在留カード number, and the 住民票 must be within 6 months of its issue date. Short term visitors in principle cannot apply, and the 茨城県警 page likewise states that, apart from exceptions, a driver's license cannot be obtained on a short term stay such as tourism. The old method of applying with a hotel address or a temporary stay certificate is no longer in the rules.

Tokyo residents follow the route on the 警視庁 page. 外免切替 is by appointment, and general applicants book through the 警視庁 administrative procedures online system. The 府中運転免許試験場 and 鮫洲運転免許試験場 accept licenses from all countries, while the 江東運転免許試験場 only accepts the 29 exempt countries and regions. Reception hours are weekdays from 8:30 in the morning to 3 in the afternoon, closed between 11 and 1. The 技能確認 is booked separately and cannot be taken the same day, so build the waiting time into your schedule. Friends with mainland China licenses who may need a retake should not fix their rental car or moving dates too firmly, leave yourself some slack. Queuing is unavoidable.

Before heading out, print the required documents PDF from the 警視庁 page and tick items off one by one, and remember to tell the counter the 住民票 needs the specified items recorded, one missing item means coming back another day. The process for Taiwan friends is relatively simple, with documents complete it is mostly a matter of queuing. Friends from mainland China should allow a longer preparation period, study the 50 question bank thoroughly, and practice the course tasks at a driving school. The two numbers, 42.8% and 13.1%, are worth pinning in front of your desk.

Quick summary

  • A Taiwan license is on the list of 29 exempt countries and regions, exempt from both the 知識確認 and the 技能確認, prepare documents and the Japanese translation per the 警視庁 list
  • A mainland China license requires the 知識確認 and 技能確認, the 知識確認 needs 45 correct out of 50 questions with a pass rate of 42.8%, and the 技能確認 pass rate is 13.1%
  • The application needs a 住民票 with the specified items recorded, valid within 6 months of issue, and short term visitors in principle cannot apply
  • Tokyo is by appointment, Fuchu and Samezu accept all countries, Koto only accepts the exemption list, and the 技能確認 is booked separately and cannot be taken the same day
  • After getting the license you must have stayed in the issuing country for a cumulative 3 months or more, this applies to both Taiwan and mainland China, so organize your passport entry and exit records early

From the editor-in-chief

A train person talking about driver's licenses has little credibility to begin with, I admit it. When I saw the 技能確認 figure of 13.1%, I took my smile back. What that number means is that barely more than one in ten people walks out of the test center smiling. I do not even know which direction the main gate of the Fuchu test center faces. I will quietly go back to squeezing onto my train.
