Limited-Time Events · AYAKA · 2026.11.17

The 2027 fukubukuro (lucky bags) are already on sale, and the Starbucks lottery deadline is November 15.

Still available at department store online shops, Starbucks lottery has ended, and there is no official news for Muji's fukukan as of mid-November.

  • Period: The special event period is from October 1, 2026, to January 31, 2027. Acceptance periods vary by retailer, and individual items may end earlier. Takashimaya Online accepts orders until January 31, Daimaru Matsuzakaya Online until December 25, and AEON Shop item deadlines fall between December 10 and 31.
  • Location / channels: mainly each company's official online store. Starbucks is only available through an official online lottery and is not sold in stores
  • Price range: AEON Shop 1,080 yen to 11,998.80 yen, Starbucks 9,500 yen (tax included, free shipping), department stores vary by product

Today is November 17. Fukubukuro is something people living in Japan have to race against time for every year. Department stores start selling online in October, lotteries close in waves through November, and if you only remember at the end of December, most of what is left is what others passed over. The rhythm is much the same this year, except one store is already too late.

Starbucks fukubukuro (lucky bags) 2027 will be sold entirely via online lottery; they are not available in stores. The application period was from 10:00 AM on November 4 to 11:59 PM on November 15, and it is now closed. If you missed it by a day, it's gone. The price is 9,500 yen, including tax and free shipping. You must be a registered 'My Starbucks' member to apply; temporary app membership does not qualify, and each person can only apply once. The lottery results will be announced on November 25, and winners must complete the purchase process by December 2, 2027. Those who were Gold members at the time of the deadline get two entries for one application—a rule introduced this year—so those who applied should keep an eye on their inbox on the day the results are announced.

Department stores are still selling, but each has a different rhythm. Takashimaya Online sells from October 1 to January 31, 2027, with items released in batches on November 25, December 9, and January 3, 2027. You can take your time browsing food, daily necessities, and children's clothing. Daimaru Matsuzakaya Online started on October 7 and runs until 10:00 AM on December 25, with food and meal vouchers released on November 2, and women's and men's clothing following on November 10. Mitsukoshi Isetan's 'New Year Lucky Bag 2027' went on sale on October 21; you need a free web membership to order. There is an additional batch of cosmetics on November 19. Delivery times are split between year-end and after the New Year, so check the delivery window carefully when choosing. Don't buy the wrong batch if you are in a rush for a gift.

🎍 AEON Shop's food lucky bags take a different route: the contents are laid out in writing, with no guessing game. Orders opened on November 1, with prices ranging from a ¥1,080 snack set to a ¥11,998.80 wagyu and seafood set, and deadlines set separately by product, with fruit mostly closing on December 10 to 17 and snacks able to go until December 31. Stock moves fast, and some items already show as sold out on the official page, so if you see a combination you like, hesitating over a weekend might mean it is gone.

Muji's fukukan (lucky cans) are something people wait for every year, but I have to be honest about this year's situation. As of November 15, the official site has not yet announced the application dates or prices for the 2027 fukukan. Most dates circulating online are just estimates based on last year's schedule. Last year's applications were from November 20 to December 1, but that was last year. This year, just wait for the official special page to update. Do not treat estimated dates as confirmed news. Once the news is announced, check the lottery eligibility, application method, and pickup stores before deciding whether to apply.

Buying fukubukuro (lucky bags) is all about the excitement of the moment you open them. With the kettle boiling and my phone on the table, I compare them one by one, add the ones I like to my list, and write the deadlines on my calendar. The lottery types are left to luck, but the department store types are still in my hands. December 25 and January 31 are the deadlines for some department stores, and AEON stops taking orders for some items in mid-December. To be safe, it is best to write the deadline found on the product page directly into your calendar for whichever one you want to buy.

From the editor-in-chief
