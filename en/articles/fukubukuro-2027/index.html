The 2027 福袋 are already on sale, and the Starbucks lottery closed on November 15

Department stores are still selling online, the Starbucks lottery has closed, and there's still no official news on MUJI's 福缶 as of mid-November

  • Period: The special event period is from October 1, 2026, to January 31, 2027. Acceptance periods vary by retailer, and individual items may end earlier. Takashimaya Online accepts orders until January 31, Daimaru Matsuzakaya Online until December 25, and AEON Shop item deadlines fall between December 10 and 31.
  • Location / channels: mainly each company's official online store. Starbucks is only available through an official online lottery and is not sold in stores
  • Price range: AEON Shop 1,080 yen to 11,998.80 yen, Starbucks 9,500 yen (tax included, free shipping), department stores vary by product

Today is November 17. Fukubukuro is something people living in Japan have to race against time for every year. Department stores start selling online in October, lotteries close in waves through November, and if you only remember at the end of December, most of what is left is what others passed over. The rhythm is much the same this year, except one store is already too late.

Starbucks 福袋 2027 is entirely by online lottery, and cannot be bought in stores. The application period was from 10:00 a.m. on November 4 to 11:59 p.m. on November 15, and has now closed. One day late and it is gone. The price is 9,500 yen, tax included and with free shipping; to apply you need to be a full member of My Starbucks, and the temporary membership in the App cannot be used, and one person can only apply once. Lottery results will be announced on November 25, and winners must complete the purchase procedure by December 2. People who were Gold members at the time of the deadline get two entries for one application, this rule is new this year, and people who applied can watch their mailbox on the day the results are announced.

Department stores are still selling, each on a different schedule. Takashimaya online runs from October 1 to January 31, 2027, with products released in batches, new batches still coming on November 25, December 9, and January 3, 2027, mixing food, daily goods, and children's clothing to browse at leisure. Daimaru Matsuzakaya online started October 7 and accepts orders until 10:00 a.m. on December 25, with food and meal vouchers released November 2 and women's and men's clothing following on November 10. Mitsukoshi Isetan's "新春福袋 2027" went on sale October 21. Ordering requires a free WEB membership. There's an additional beauty release on November 19, and delivery times are split between year-end delivery and after-New-Year delivery, so check the delivery window carefully when choosing, and if you're in a hurry to give it as a gift, don't buy the wrong batch.

🎍 AEON Shop's food lucky bags take a different route: the contents are laid out in writing, with no guessing game. Orders opened on November 1, with prices ranging from a ¥1,080 snack set to a ¥11,998.80 wagyu and seafood set, and deadlines set separately by product, with fruit mostly closing on December 10 to 17 and snacks able to go until December 31. Stock moves fast, and some items already show as sold out on the official page, so if you see a combination you like, hesitating over a weekend might mean it is gone.

People wait for MUJI's 福缶 every year. To be honest about this year's situation: as of November 15, the official application dates and price for the 2027 福缶 have not yet been announced. The dates circulating online are mostly estimated based on last year's schedule. Last year's application period was November 20 to December 1, but that was last year. This year, wait for the official special page to be updated, and don't treat estimated dates as confirmed information. After the information is announced, first confirm the lottery eligibility, application method, and pickup store, then decide whether to apply.

What you buy with a 福袋 is that feeling at the moment you open it. Water boiling in the kettle, phone on the table, comparing shop by shop slowly, adding favorites to a list, writing deadlines into the calendar. The lottery type is already left to luck, the department store type is still in your own hands; December 25 and January 31 are deadlines for some department stores, and AEON closes orders for some products in mid-December. For whichever one you want to buy, it's safer to note the deadline on the product page directly into your calendar.
