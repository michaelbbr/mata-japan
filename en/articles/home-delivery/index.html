Came home from work to another delivery notice? Do not miss the holding periods for Japanese deliveries

After setting up 置き配, when you come home and open the door, the parcel is already waiting for you in the 玄関

A little after seven in the evening, after swaying on the train all the way home, still carrying a convenience store bag in hand, you open the mailbox and an absence notification slip is lying inside. The driver has long gone. For office workers living in Japan, this scene plays out several times every month.

When you get the slip, calling to arrange 再配達 is a normal reaction, and you can also arrange it online or via LINE, but scanning the QR code on the slip is the fastest. What fewer people pay attention to is the number of days: parcels do not stay long at the delivery office. For regular Kuroneko 宅急便 the storage period is 7 days, and for クール宅急便 it is only 3 days. Sagawa's 再配達 application period is 8 days, and Japan Post's general parcel storage period is 7 days. Each company's counting method and applicable conditions may differ, and the most accurate deadline is still the one on the absence slip and the official tracking page. Once the deadline passes, the parcel is returned to the sender; for online shopping you can still negotiate with the seller again, but for things sent from home or official documents that are returned, the cost and time of resending are your own.

By the time I saw the ticket it was already past 7 p.m., and if you want to arrange delivery again that same day, it's usually too late. With Kuroneko, applying online or by automated voice, the cutoff for same-day delivery is around 5:40 p.m., varying by area. 7 to 9 p.m. is a time when people are more likely to be home after work, so many office workers may want to choose that slot. But if you've already missed the application cutoff that day, it's more practical to reschedule for the next day.

Later I switched to 置き配. From June 2024, Kuroneko members can specify 置き配 for regular 宅急便 and 宅急便コンパクト; when the shipping notification pops up, select the placement location in the app, and the parcel will be placed where you chose: in front of the entrance, in a delivery box, in the gas meter box, in the garage, in a storage room, or you can also choose to have the building manager receive it. You don't need to be home to wait. Parcels that are refrigerated or frozen, cash on delivery, or collection on delivery are not eligible; those still require you to sign in person. Some cards sent by banks, or mail such as 簡易書留 sent by government agencies, also cannot be treated directly as regular parcels for 置き配. This type of mail usually needs to be delivered in person with receipt confirmed, and whether it is restricted to the addressee depends on the actual type of mail.

Apartment residents get stuck at another hurdle. In complexes with auto-lock front doors, drivers cannot enter the lobby and packages cannot reach your door; some complexes' management rules explicitly prohibit leaving packages in hallways; if the delivery box is full, the driver can only take the package away again. If 置き配 is not convenient at your apartment, see whether this package can be changed to convenience store pickup, or use a PUDO delivery locker. Not every package is supported, so check in the delivery company's notice or app first to be safe. Pick it up on your way home, without racing against time.

📦 In a 国土交通省 survey, the redelivery rate in April 2024 was about 10.4%, meaning one in ten parcels had to make a second trip, and the government target at the time was to bring that figure down to 6%. News about driver shortages has been around for years; my habit now is simple: as soon as a shipping notification pops up, I change the time, and if I cannot wait around, I choose 置き配. When I get home and open the mailbox, it is clean, with no delivery notice slip.

Quick summary

  • Kuroneko holds for 7 days, クール宅急便 only 3 days; Sagawa redelivery requests within 8 days, Japan Post holds for 7 days (counting methods differ by company; follow the delivery notice and official pages)
  • Online applications for same-day redelivery: Kuroneko cutoff is around 17:40, varies by area
  • Kuroneko members can specify 置き配, and it can also be used for regular 宅急便; refrigerated and frozen, cash on delivery, and payment collection are not eligible
  • If the apartment auto-lock keeps drivers out and the delivery box is full, having it sent to a convenience store near your company or a PUDO locker is more reliable

From the editor-in-chief

My delivery notice once lay in my mailbox until it expired, and the parcel was sent back. The sender was my family back home, and my mother called to ask if I had moved. Now I've learned to photograph the slip as soon as I see it; the deadline on it is far more reliable than my memory.
