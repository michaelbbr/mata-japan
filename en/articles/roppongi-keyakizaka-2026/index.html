Roppongi Keyakizaka's blue and white light display runs until Christmas—perfect for a post-work stroll.

ROPPONGI HILLS CHRISTMAS 2026: Runs until December 25. About 970,000 LEDs light up the 400-meter Keyakizaka street. 17:00 to 22:00, free.

- Period: November 5 to December 25, 2026 - Time: 17:00 to 22:00 - Location: Roppongi Hills Keyakizaka-dori (6-10-1 Roppongi, Minato Ward, Tokyo) - Cost: Free, viewable directly from the street - Christmas Market: November 21 to December 25, 2026, 11:00 to 21:00, inside the Roppongi Hills complex

Keyakizaka is a gentle downward slope. Looking from the top of the slope toward Azabu-Juban, the two rows of zelkova trees are neatly lit with blue and white lights, and Tokyo Tower stands perfectly in front of the slope. The orange tower is wrapped in cool-toned lights, creating a color contrast so sharp it's almost unreasonable. 🌲

This year's theme is SNOW & BLUE. About 970,000 white and blue LEDs line the 400-meter slope, and when the wind blows, the entire canopy of trees seems to shimmer. The lights are on from November 5 to Christmas Day, December 25, every night from 17:00 to 22:00. It's very convenient for those in Tokyo—just a few minutes' walk from Roppongi Station on the Hibiya Line. It's an easy stop for office workers to enjoy on their way home.

Most photographers crowd the pedestrian bridge in the middle of the slope and the top. The peak time is between 5:00 PM and 10:00 PM on weekends, where you'll have to queue for a spot on the bridge. It's much less crowded right after the lights turn on during weekday evenings. The sky still holds a deep blue, and the orange of the tower and the blue-and-white of the zelkova trees appear in the same frame—those are the most colorful minutes of the day on this slope. If you want a leisurely stroll, go after 7:00 PM on a weekday from the top of the slope; the crowds thin out, you can slow your pace, and the cold air is filled with the sweet scent of mulled wine drifting over from the market.

The Christmas market runs from November 21 to December 25, from 11:00 AM to 9:00 PM. After seeing the lights, walk over to buy a hot drink to warm up your fingers, then take a stroll around the Mori Garden area. The 66 Plaza and Mori Garden also have their own illuminations, with dates and times slightly different from Keyakizaka, so check the official website before you head out. Once December 25 passes, the Keyakizaka lights are taken down. The short 'shelf life' of this slope is part of why people want to visit every year.
