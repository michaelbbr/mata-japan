The old health insurance card is still lying in my wallet, but the card you need to bring to the hospital has already changed

The old health insurance card expired on December 1, 2025. Now, when visiting a doctor, bring your MyNa Insurance Card or your Eligibility Confirmation Certificate. If you haven't processed this, the Eligibility Confirmation Certificate will be mailed to your home automatically.

Many people still have that old health insurance card in their wallets. Even if the date printed on the card hasn't passed, it's useless. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's announcement is very direct: new health insurance cards stopped being issued on December 2, 2024, and the old cards are only valid until December 1, 2025. After that day, they are finished. From December 2, 2025, hospital and pharmacy counters will only recognize two things: the My Number Card that has been registered for health insurance use (the 'Myna Insurance Card') and the Eligibility Certificate. You can keep the old card as a souvenir, but you cannot use it to see a doctor.

Using the My Number health insurance card differs from the old card by only about ten seconds at the counter. Place the card on the facial recognition reader at the medical institution or pharmacy, follow the screen to confirm your identity, and your eligibility is checked on that machine. Once you agree, the medical institution can access some of your medical and medication information within the scope of the system. It is more convenient when switching clinics temporarily, but you still need to actively tell the doctor about your current symptoms, important medical history, and medications you are taking. The reader beeps and it is over; first-time users are usually stunned because it is faster than expected. The counter staff will usually stand nearby and point at the screen to guide you through it once.

The Eligibility Confirmation Certificate is for those who have not yet used the MyNa Insurance Card. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare stated that this is issued for free during the transition period and does not require an application; your medical insurer will mail it directly. Recipients include those who have not yet obtained a My Number Card, those who have the card but have not registered it for health insurance use, those who have applied to cancel their registration, and those whose card's electronic certificate has expired. The design and issuance format vary by insurer; National Health Insurance, health insurance associations, and mutual aid associations each do their own thing, so it is normal for it to look different from your neighbor's. Do not throw away the envelope. The validity period is set by the insurer within a range of up to five years; whatever is printed on the card is the expiration date. Check the date as soon as you receive it and don't let it get buried in the back of a drawer.

📋 There are two situations where you must apply yourself. People who lost their card or are in the process of renewal, as well as the elderly, those with disabilities, or those who have difficulty using a card reader and require special assistance, must apply to their insurer. Those who require special assistance will not need to apply again during future renewals. For those under the Late-Stage Elderly Healthcare System, there was a temporary arrangement where everyone was issued a Certificate of Eligibility valid until the end of July 2026, regardless of whether they had a MyNa Insurance Card. This universal issuance has ended. From August 2026, it is handled by age: those 85 and older will automatically receive a new certificate; those 84 and younger will have their status determined by their MyNa Insurance Card usage. Frequent users will receive a 'Qualification Information Notice' (which cannot be used alone for medical care and must be used with the MyNa Insurance Card), while infrequent users will receive a Certificate of Eligibility. The criteria vary slightly by regional union, so if you have elderly family members, help them check which document was in that July envelope.

For those who haven't done it yet, just go to the hospital with your qualification confirmation document. The process is exactly the same as handing over your old health insurance card; the reception staff will not give you a hard time. If you do not have either card on hand, call your insurer before you leave. For National Health Insurance, ask at the National Health Insurance counter at your ward office or city hall. For company health insurance, ask your health insurance association or HR department. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's website also states that matters regarding the qualification confirmation document are the responsibility of the insurance provider you are enrolled in. You are already tired from having a cold or fever; don't wait until you reach the counter to realize you brought the wrong card. Most people living here know that awkward feeling of rummaging through your wallet in front of the reception desk.

Quick summary

  • The old health insurance card expired on December 1, 2025. From December 2, 2025, please bring your MyNa Insurance Card (My Number Card linked to health insurance) or your Eligibility Confirmation Certificate when visiting a doctor.
  • For those who meet the general criteria for automatic issuance, the eligibility confirmation document will be mailed by the insurer for free; some special cases require an individual application.
  • The validity period of the Eligibility Confirmation Certificate is set by the insurer within a maximum of five years; check the date on the card when you receive it.
  • People whose card is lost, being renewed, or who need consideration due to difficulty using a card reader must apply themselves to their insurer
  • The My Number health insurance card uses a card reader with facial recognition at the counter to verify eligibility; once you agree, you can share your past medical and medication history.
