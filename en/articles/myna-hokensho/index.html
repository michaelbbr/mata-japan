The old health insurance card is still lying in my wallet, but the card you need to bring to the hospital has already changed

The old 健康保険証 expired on December 1, 2025; now bring your マイナ保険証 or 資格確認書 when seeing a doctor, and for those who haven't applied, the 資格確認書 will be mailed to your home on its own

Many people still keep that old health insurance card in their wallet. Even if the date printed on the card hasn't arrived yet, it's no use. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's announcement is very direct: issuance of new old-style 健康保険証 stopped on December 2, 2024, and the validity of old cards on hand lasts at most until December 1, 2025, after which they end. From December 2, 2025, hospital and pharmacy counters accept two other things: a マイナンバーカード that has completed health insurance card use registration, that is, a マイナ保険証, and a 資格確認書. You can keep the old card as a souvenir, but you can't use it to see a doctor.

How you use the マイナ保険証 differs from the old card in those ten-odd seconds at the counter. You place the card on the facial-recognition card reader at the reception of a medical institution or pharmacy, follow the screen to verify your identity, and your eligibility is checked on that machine. With your consent, medical institutions can, within the scope supported by the system, refer to some of your medical visit and medication information. It is more convenient when you suddenly change clinics, but you should still proactively tell the doctor about your current symptoms, important medical history, and the medicines you are taking. The reader beeps and it is over; first-time users are usually taken aback for a moment, because it is faster than expected, and counter staff will usually stand beside you and guide you through the screen once.

The 資格確認書 is for people who are not yet using a マイナ保険証. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare states that it is issued free of charge during the transition period and that you do not need to apply yourself; your health insurance insurer will send it directly. It covers people who have not yet obtained a マイナンバーカード, people who have a card but have not registered it for use as a health insurance card, people who have applied to cancel that registration, and people whose card's electronic certificate has expired. The style and form of issue differ by insurer; 國民健康保險, health insurance societies, and mutual aid associations each make their own, so it is normal if yours looks different from your neighbor's. Do not throw away the envelope. The validity period is set by the insurer within a range of up to five years, and whatever is printed on the card is what counts, so take a look at the date as soon as you receive it and do not let it sink to the back of a drawer.

📋 There are two situations where you need to apply yourself. People who have lost their card or are in the process of renewing it, and people who have difficulty using a card reader and need consideration, such as older people or people with disabilities, will be issued one after applying to their insurer; people who need consideration do not need to apply again at later renewals. For people under the latter-stage elderly medical care system, there was an interim arrangement for a while where everyone was first issued a 資格確認書 valid until the end of July 2026, regardless of whether they had a マイナ保険証. That blanket issuance has ended, and from August 2026 it changes to age-based handling: for people aged 85 and over, the new 資格確認書 will still be sent automatically; for people aged 84 and under, it depends on their use of the マイナ保険証, those who use it regularly will receive a 「資格情報のお知らせ」, which alone cannot be used for medical care and must be used together with the マイナ保険証, while those who use it less will receive a 資格確認書. How the division is made differs slightly by each 広域連合, so if you have an older family member, help check which of the two was actually in that July letter.

People who have not completed the procedures yet can just bring their 資格確認書 when they go to a hospital now; it is exactly the same action as handing over the old card, and the counter will not make things difficult. If you do not have either card at hand yet, call your insurer before going out; for 國保, ask the 國保 counter at the 区役所 or city office, for company health insurance ask the health insurance society or HR, and the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare page says the same, that matters concerning the 資格確認書 are handled by the insurer you belong to. Being sick with a cold and fever is tiring enough; do not arrive at the counter only to find you brought the wrong card, that embarrassment of searching through your wallet in front of reception is something most people living here understand.

Quick summary

  • The old 健康保険証 expired on December 1, 2025; from December 2, 2025, bring your マイナ保険証 or 資格確認書 when seeing a doctor
  • For people who meet the general conditions for issuance without application, the 資格確認書 is sent free of charge by the insurer; some special cases require applying yourself.
  • The validity period of the 資格確認書 is set by the insurer within five years; check the date on the card when you receive it
  • People whose card is lost, being renewed, or who need consideration due to difficulty using a card reader must apply themselves to their insurer
  • With the マイナ保険証, your eligibility is confirmed at the counter using a card reader with facial recognition, and with your consent, information about your past medical visits and medications can be shared
