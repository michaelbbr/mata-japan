Tokyo Dome City's illuminations stay lit until midnight, so you can see them no matter how late you get off work

TOKYO SNOW DOME CITY, 102 days, over 1 million lights, illuminations free to view

  • Period: November 19, 2026 (Thu) to February 28, 2027 (Sun), 102 days in total
  • Lighting: 17:00 to 24:00, free, no reservation required
  • Location: All of Tokyo Dome City (1-3-61 Koraku, Bunkyo City, Tokyo), about a 3-minute walk from Suidobashi Station and Korakuen Station

Living in Tokyo through winter, you do not need to specially schedule illuminations. The Suidobashi and Korakuen area is a transfer point for many people to begin with. At five in the evening the lights come on, the sky has just darkened, and walking out of the station toward the Dome, the road starts to have light, strings of white lights glowing, looking especially clear in the cold air. Tokyo Dome City's winter illuminations this year start on November 19, named TOKYO SNOW DOME CITY, now in its 22nd edition, with a theme of snow and snow globes, a main color of white, and more than 1 million light bulbs used across the whole park.

On the lawn plaza of Central Park stands a WONDER SNOW TREE about 15 meters tall, changing color with music and visuals, with a show every 15 minutes. Standing underneath and watching, the light falls on your face, and after one show ends, you want to wait for the next. On the LaQua side there is a LUMINOUS SNOW DOME 8 meters in diameter, where you can experience bell ringing inside; from a distance it looks like a large snowball that has landed on the ground. This year there is also a collaboration with Cinnamoroll, and you can find his displays in the complex; the collaboration park at Gallery AaMo charges a separate fee, with ticket prices on the official website, so just check before you go.

The lights stay on until 24:00, but the tree performance only runs until 21:15, and the snow globe bell-ringing experience ends at 21:00. If you arrive after 21:15, the main tree performance is already over and only the static lights remain, though the illuminations themselves stay lit until 24:00. If you want to avoid crowds, try a later time slot on a weekday evening. Whether there will be a line on site depends on the day. Before you head out, though, it's best to glance at the Dome's event schedule for that day. If you happen to coincide with a concert letting out, you'll instead run into a whole crowd pouring out just after 21:00.

✨ The period is long, 102 days running through the end of February, so during Christmas and the year-end weeks you do not have to squeeze in with tourists. After January, pick a weekday evening, hold a hot drink in your hands, take a walk around, and then go home.

The puzzle-solving stamp rally and Gallery AaMo's big-eared dog collaboration amusement area are only until January 17, 2027; the illuminations themselves run until the end of February, those two end earlier. For people bringing children, plan your itinerary starting with those two, and take your time with the lights.

The return trip is also easy to arrange. The LaQua shopping mall is right nearby; if you want to have dinner or shop along the way, check the shops' opening hours first. From there, walking to Korakuen Station or Suidobashi Station is convenient either way. The area around the Dome has a lot of open space and strong wind, cold on your face, so remember to bring a scarf and gloves. The illuminations stay lit until 24:00, but last trains differ by line, so people going late should check the return trip first.

From the editor-in-chief

Standing in front of that tree waiting for the show, one show every 15 minutes, and when I arrived one had just ended. I had to wait another 15 minutes for the next one, so I stood in the cold wind with my hands in my pockets, telling myself this was winter romance. When the lights changed color, I felt the wait was worth it.
