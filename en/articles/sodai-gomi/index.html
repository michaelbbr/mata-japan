If you want to throw away old furniture before the end of the year, you need to book 粗大ごみ in December

The reception center is closed from December 29, and reception slots for within the year fill up quickly in every ward

Chinese people living in Japan often have to do major cleaning twice: once in December following Japan's year-end and New Year period, and once again before Lunar New Year because they want to clean again themselves; it cannot be avoided. For things cleared out, small items are sorted and discarded following the usual rules. Large items such as wardrobes, bookshelves, mattresses, and bicycles usually have to be handled as 粗大垃圾. Using Shinjuku Ward and Setagaya Ward, checked for this article, as examples, anything with a side longer than 30 cm is classified as 粗大垃圾, and size and item type must be judged according to each ward's rules; other places cannot apply this directly.

You cannot just carry 粗大ごみ out to the roadside and leave it for someone to collect. You have to apply to the reception center for the ward you live in, by phone or online; when you apply, the collection date is set and the fee is calculated at the same time. Amounts are graded by item and size; in Setagaya Ward and Shinjuku Ward fees are in the range of 400 to 3,200 yen. Payment is by processing stickers, sold at convenience stores and supermarkets displaying a sign saying "有料粗大ごみ処理券取扱所"; in Setagaya Ward it's a 200-yen A sticker combined with a 300-yen B sticker to make up the fee amount. Write the collection date and your name on the sticker, stick it in a visible place on the item, and move it to the designated place before 8 a.m. on collection day; you don't need to wait there, they collect it according to the list.

What really traps people at the end of the year is time. Shinjuku City's announcement this year is very clear: phone reception for bulky waste is closed from December 29 to January 3, and Setagaya City's phone reception is also closed during the same period. Online applications are still available 24 hours, but collection itself also stops for a few days over the year-end and New Year period. Even if you can book, there are only a few days left in the year when collection is possible, and everyone who applies after mid-December crowds together, so the date you get often jumps to January already. A compilation of year-end and New Year waste collection announcements from 50 cities shows that among the 29 cities with phone reception data, 21 stopped accepting applications from December 29 to January 3, and the deadline for collection within the year generally falls in late December. If you want to clear out old furniture within the year, complete your application between late November and early December, and you'll have more choice of dates.

The dates differ slightly in each of Tokyo's 23 wards. This article only checked Shinjuku and Setagaya, so don't apply it directly to other wards. The way to check your own ward is straightforward: go to your ward office website and search for "年末年始 粗大ごみ", find this year's page, and it will say until what date phone reception is closed and how the last collection of the year is arranged. The ward's public relations paper December issue prints the same schedule. Before you throw away the copy that lands in your mailbox, just flip to the garbage section.

🧹 People who did not apply in time are easily drawn in by flyers in their mailbox. Flyers that say free collection and same-day pickup, where one phone call brings them over, need caution. Household garbage can only be collected by ward-licensed operators and commissioned operators, as the Setagaya Ward website states directly. Unlicensed operators who take things away may dump them where no one knows, in the mountains or by rivers, and when responsibility is traced it may come back to the person who paid to dispose of the items; disputes where collection was said to be free but a high fee was added after loading onto the truck also happen every year. If you really need a private operator, before you hire one check that they have a ward license, and do not rely only on the phone number on the flyer.

The big cleanup is scheduled for the end of November; the bulky waste has been booked, so in December only small things like wiping the windows are left, and you can take your time.

Quick summary

  • 粗大ごみ is only collected if you apply; book by phone or online, and the collection date and fee are decided at the time of application
  • Buy the processing stickers at convenience stores or supermarkets, write the collection date and your name and stick them on, and put the item at the designated place before 8 a.m. that day
  • In Shinjuku Ward and Setagaya Ward, telephone reception is closed from December 29 to January 3, and slots for collection within the year are already very full in December
  • For your own ward's deadline, search "年末年始 粗大ごみ" on the ward office official website and check this year's announcement
  • When you see a flyer for free collection, don't rush to call. First check whether the operator has the household waste collection permit required in your area, or is a contractor commissioned by the municipality
