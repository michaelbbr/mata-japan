Going to sing カラオケ alone, in Japan you really do not need to feel embarrassed

An older guy's second stop on the weekend: an introduction to ヒトカラ (solo karaoke)

Many Chinese friends who have just moved to Japan ask me the same question: 'If I walk into a karaoke shop alone, will the staff think I'm weird?'

There's no need to worry at all. Japan has long had the term 'ヒトカラ' for singing karaoke alone, and many chains accept single-person use, with some shops even offering dedicated booths or plans. Large chains such as ビッグエコー offer weekday daytime plans for one person. Many shops charge per person per 30 minutes, and there are also free-time plans. Weekday daytime is usually cheaper, but single-person rates and drink rules vary by shop, so it's best to check the price list on that shop's official page before you go.

Why do people living here love ヒトカラ? Because it solves the most practical stress-relief problem of living in Japan: no need to gather a group, no need to accommodate others, and you can sing the same song five times in a row until you've learned it. After work, walk into a カラオケ shop around stations where office workers gather, like Shinbashi or Ikebukuro, pick songs alone and sing alone, with no need at all to consider anyone else's playlist. Everyone is singing away the week's fatigue in their own way.

🎤 If it is your first time, just go on a weekday during the day or for an early evening session; the prices are friendliest and you are less likely to find the place full and be turned away. When you enter, ask clearly whether there is a solo surcharge (お一人様料金) and whether you need to order one drink (ワンオーダー制); rules differ between chains and the total at checkout can differ a lot. Book in advance with the official app and check coupons before you go; many chains' app member offers are better value than walk-in prices.

The songs I sing most often myself are still old songs. Singing is like running: there is no need to compete with anyone. After singing, you walk out of the shop, the Tokyo night wind blows, and you can go back to facing Monday again. This piece is fine, you all go and sing once too, then come back and leave a comment telling me what you sang.
