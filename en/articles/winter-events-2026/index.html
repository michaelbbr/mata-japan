2026 Tokyo & Metropolitan Area Winter Event Guide: Christmas markets, illuminations, and limited-time events—check the dates on one page.

A roundup of winter-only events, mostly in the Tokyo area: Christmas markets, illuminations, and lucky bags. Each event lists its dates and the one highlight worth seeing. Newly verified events will be added as we check them.

Living in Japan, the classic winter mistake is showing up somewhere only to find out the event ended yesterday. This page collects the winter events we've verified on the site, so you can check the dates first and then pick your weekend. New events get added once verified, sorted by end date — the ones ending soonest come first.

Winter events list (sorted by end date)

Tokyo Christmas Market: Shin-Toyosu and Shiba Park venues

It's not at Jingu Gaien this year; the Shin-Toyosu venue opens November 20, and the Shiba Park venue starts December 4. Both venues run until December 25. Adult admission starts at 1,000 yen on weekdays. Check the venue before you go so you don't end up at the wrong place.

Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse: Seaside Christmas Market

From November 20 to December 25, entry to the main venue starts at 500 yen, and from December 5, it stays open until 10 PM. There's also a free area around Red Brick Warehouse Park, perfect for a leisurely train trip on the weekend.

Ebisu Garden Place: Baccarat chandelier and Christmas market

The Baccarat crystal chandelier is lit during the day, from 11:00 to 23:00, and will be on display from November 14, 2026, to January 11, 2027. The Christmas market only runs until December 25, so those who want to visit the market should go before then.

Meguro River Winter Sakura

From December 4, 2026, to January 31, 2027, the 2.2-kilometer stretch on both sides will be illuminated in pink, free of charge. The electricity for the lights is generated from biodiesel made from recycled cooking oil, so it's easy to stop by on your way home from work.

2027 Lucky Bag Roundup

Each company has different lottery and sales times. The Starbucks lottery has already ended, but department store online lucky bags are still available. Check the contents and deadlines carefully before buying. Acceptance periods vary, with the latest ending on January 31, 2027, though individual items may end sooner.

Marunouchi: Gyoko-dori and Naka-dori — two illuminated streets

Gyoko-dori is lit only until December 25, while Naka-dori stays lit until February 14. The only window when both are lit is November 27 to December 25. If you want to see both in one go, go before Christmas.

Tokyo Dome City TOKYO SNOW DOME CITY

From November 19, 2026, to February 28, 2027, the lights will be on until midnight. With 102 days and over 1 million lights, you'll have plenty of time to visit even if you get off work late. If you go in January, it will be much less crowded.

Verified winter events will be added to this page as they are confirmed. If you are looking for a specific event, just check the dates on this page.

Quick summary

  • Check the end dates first: most Christmas markets end on December 25; illuminations usually run to the end of January or into February
  • Free illuminations: Meguro River, Tokyo Dome City, and Marunouchi — all viewable on a stroll, no ticket needed
  • To dodge the crowds, go on a weekday evening in January. The lights are still on and there are far fewer people

From the editor-in-chief

The Ebisu market ends December 25. If I wander over on the 26th, all I'll see is stalls being taken down. This page is for checking the dates before planning the trip — and I'm the first one who benefits.
